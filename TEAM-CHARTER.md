# PLP Group 33 - Team Charter

## 1. Communication
We will use WhatsApp for daily communication.

## 2. Response Time
Members should respond within 12 hours where possible.

## 3. Meetings
Meetings will be arranged based on team availability.

## 4. Task Assignment
Every task must have an owner, priority, and clear Definition of Done.

## 5. Task Updates
Members must update their task status on the same day they work on it.

## 6. Commit Messages
We will use clear commit messages in this format:

<type>: <what changed> - <why it matters>

## 7. Inactivity
If a member has no visible activity for 2 days, the team will contact them and follow the agreed escalation process.

## 8. Conflict Resolution
Disagreements will first be discussed respectfully as a team.

## 9. Team Leader
The team leader will coordinate deadlines, meetings, and final submission.
